How we score standing

ACT rates each company across five due-diligence dimensions — Profitability, Growth, Cash & liquidity, Financial stability, Trustworthiness — read from the analysis feed (SSM filings + the analysis layer) and benchmarked against what's typical. The overall word blends the five; any dimension we can't evidence is shown as "no data" rather than guessed.

Qualitative reads (moat, market, risks) are extracted from the filings — confidence varies. Figures may be unit-inconsistent at source. ACT presents data and meaning; the decision is yours.
